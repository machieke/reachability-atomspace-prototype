# Online numerical PLN/lifecycle conformance v1

Prerequisite publication: `4fffa740003a121eb4fd7809c143bbdaa22419fa`.
This closes that comparison without tuning pressure. This new increment is an
engineering conformance slice, not an inference efficiency or planning contest.

The runtime will live in `experimental_online_pln`, preserving all frozen
`reachability`, `experimental_pressure`, `experimental_planning` and prior review
bytes. It uses existing SQLite authority, numerical certificates, native PLN,
all-current-exact-literal/v1 decisions and deployment/goal APIs. AtomSpace is a
disposable readback-checked projection. No native dependency fallback is allowed.

Selection is FIFO by the enumeration round when a logical item first becomes
ready, with canonical semantic operation/target/input identity ties. There is
no pressure, confidence-weighted utility, future answer or native ranking call.
Fresh authority bindings are required at execution; queue age confers no permit.
Fixed-basis failed attempts are suppressed; changed relevant inputs or explicit
new acquisition opportunities permit another bounded attempt. Every attempt is
charged. No crash continuation for this new coordinator is claimed.

Bounds: 16 registered rules, 32 current estimates, 64 candidates, 128 tuple
visits per enumeration, 32 selected operations, 64 declared work units and 16
acquisition units per episode. Inputs exceeding size caps are rejected; a
truncated candidate frontier stops explicitly. Budget exhaustion retains open
obligations, not a proof of impossibility. Deterministic fixtures use seed 0.

The twelve predeclared fixtures in `fixtures.json` are siblings of one synthetic
deployment scenario, not independent performance samples. Initial received
reports are adopted through the ledger during setup; no initial derived forecast
is supplied. Future events are evaluator-private. Closed-loop work comes from
the public frontier. Exact-boundary injected changes are separately marked seam
tests. The original deployment benchmark and static planner are unchanged.

The live-choice pair starts with two ready auxiliary deductions and one incomplete
deployment deduction sharing premises. A numeric acquisition capability competes
with both ready deductions. Its missing report can unlock the deployment route.
Other families use a smaller subset of the same grounded scenario to isolate
lineage, replacement, numerical boundary, dispatch and monitoring behavior.

Expected observable prefixes (checked, not controller inputs):

- Before any product/sample observation, external world loss and goal outstanding
  remain ten, even with a supported forecast, a PASS decision or an ACK.
- Valid selected deductions have five ordered inputs, eight-cell joint witnesses,
  matching native/checker proposals and issued pre/post/commit PASS.
- Independent revision produces an additional current estimate. Its low-confidence
  parents remain current and keep the all-current gate UNKNOWN. Copied roots cannot
  produce a revised estimate or increased confidence.
- Revocation after proposal makes that old commit STALE. An equal-valued new report
  or retained independent alternative can only support a fresh transition.
- The known near-one joint counterexample fails pre-admission without a native
  call or repaired output; the finite valid sibling passes.
- A relevant revocation after dispatch preparation blocks final send as STALE;
  the unchanged-basis sibling is accepted. No receipt or coverage implies relief.
- Correct product and samples at three distinct successive ticks produce losses
  ten, ten, zero. Wrong-product observations fail. A later unhealthy sample reopens
  ten units while preserving BUILT history. Forecast retirement after ACK does not
  erase the accepted effect or prevent supported observed completion.

Tests also cover immutable enumeration, rediscovery/exact retry, harmless rename
and reorder, unchanged accepted operations under agenda-order changes, hard-fact
separation, stale proposals and candidates, alternatives, explicit bounds and
native errors/mismatch. A few test-only mutants target these new seams.

Both finite-checker and pinned native modes run the same fixtures. Projection and
reopening occur at quiescent boundaries with no native re-inference. Traces retain
complete public frontiers, exact revisions, certificates, proposals, actual calls,
receipts, current/retired views, goal accounting and stopping reasons. Timings
include enumeration, acquisition, runtime startup/call, pre/post, commit, dispatch,
monitoring, retries, projection and total; nested/inclusive costs are identified.
Setup, trace serialization, SQL and overhead must not disappear from total costs.

Source/config hashes, exact regression suites/counts and omissions accompany one
review bundle. Failed runs are retained before fixes and any fixture correction
requires an explicit explanation. Favorable and blocked cases both remain visible.
No general statistical or performance claim follows from these fixtures.
