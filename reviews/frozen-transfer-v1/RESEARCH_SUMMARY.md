The unchanged integrated loop executed acquisition, certified numerical deduction,
complete producer review, guarded deployment and outcome monitoring on twelve new
small dependency structures. This is evidence within its existing fragment and
world, with no seeded intermediate beliefs and no controller tuning on these
parents. The source was frozen at `c28a7dc`; the cohort was committed at `8dac6c7`.

All 24 executions conformed. Both retrieval arms produced the same complete
semantic histories on all twelve parents. Per arm, six parents reached recognized
completion during the episode and five retained recognized success at the end.
Seven reached physical completion and six retained physical success. The five

dispatched. A conformance pass is not a task-success label.

## Favorable, neutral and unfavorable findings

- Favorable capability evidence: A1–A4 acquired missing primitive inputs and
  executed two- or three-hop native deductions, including shared prerequisites,
  alternative intermediate producers and complete required final review. C1
  replaced support through public admission, retired affected ancestry and
  completed after fresh deductions. Exact current support and authority gates
  survived all public changes. All 639 applicable regressions passed.
- Neutral comparison: scan and full-session native retrieval used the same
  consumer and made the same semantic choices. Neither is an independent
  cognitive comparator. Equal outcomes establish bounded retrieval equivalence,
  not the benefit of pressure, optimal reasoning or general intelligence.
- Unfavorable effectiveness: B1 remained incomplete after an unavailable report;
  B2 retained an applicability objection from copied lineage; B3 finished its
  numerical applications with insufficient confidence; B4 ran out of permitted
  pre-dispatch acquisition capacity. C2 acquired adverse evidence and remained
  blocked. These outcomes are retained without calling every unresolved task a
  correct abstention or a proven missed opportunity.
- Unfavorable monitoring: A2 and C1 recognized completion two ticks after physical
  completion. C3 never obtained a product observation. C4 lost completion and
  received the first confirming adverse health sample one tick after regression.
  Recognition depends on admitted observations and their freshness, not inference
  confidence alone.
- Unfavorable engineering result: native retrieval was slower in every paired
  parent. Summed episode time was **131.041007 seconds native versus 83.003540
  seconds scan**, a descriptive difference of 48.037467 seconds. Both executed
  52 actual native formula calls. There was one counterbalanced pass, no timing
  significance claim, and no post-result optimization.

Per arm, the summed physical loss integral was 710 and recognized loss integral
810. The difference is located at A2 (20), C1 (20), and C3 (60). This is an
accounting decomposition under the fixed logical clock. It is not a measured
causal effect of a different scheduler, query engine, pressure score or sensor.
Equal logical losses do not establish equal responsiveness in wall-clock time.
