# Shared protected assembly v1 — premeasurement contract

Preserve publication 71c32df and measured/auditor 0134092, all prior implementations,
protocols, failures, archives, formulas, native lifetimes/checks and hard gates.
This is a separate shared minimum-service coordinator, not a transport promotion.

First reproduce the frozen alternatives boundary at capacity 48, q16/q48, all three
orderers, finite/native formulas (12 diagnostic runs). Analyze whole tranches;
known producers, affordable attempts, complete tuples and authority are distinct.

Two contracts (frozen scheduling, protected assembly) × queue/local/flow × two
query budgets (16/48) × finite/native formulas × eight parents = 192 executions.
Use the existing 48-entry/512-KiB workspace, chosen mechanically to fit ordinary
exact bundles while still permitting eviction. Work 16, acquisitions 16, selections
32; all response, tuple, field, job, byte and source/backing bounds are unchanged.
Seed 0. Rotate six orderer permutations, alternate contract/formula orders. One
pass; timings descriptive and concurrent validation load recorded. No parameter
sweep, capacity search, new fallback or early goal-stop policy.

Two diagnostic parents are unchanged alternatives and shared-intermediate. Six
additional engineering structures, fixed before new-policy execution:

1. ready-broad: route-control plus three later FIFO forecast producers with fresh
   middle literals and missing inputs. The original ready route remains unchanged.
2. missing-upstream: path-two plus another producer for its intermediate, with a
   fresh middle literal. Existing missing evidence uses its actual observation port.
3. unproductive-early: route-control plus an earlier FIFO forecast producer whose
   three additional input reports have confidence .05, and an unadopted direct
   forecast report (.7,.8). A premature low-confidence deduction can make all-current
   acceptance conservative; the received report may be discovered first otherwise.
   This is a deliberately adverse scheduling control, not a promised improvement.
4. evicted-alternatives: path-alternatives plus five independent equal-valued reports
   for the shared first premise. More exact alternatives create retention and tuple
   work within the existing global fragment limits; no capacity changes.
5. refreshed-competing: change-replacement plus a second initial forecast producer.
   Retain the equal-valued revocation/replacement boundary and later new producer.
6. monitor-competition: supported-reopen with wrong first product and three missing
   forecast producers, retaining actual health samples and later failure.

These are eight parent structures, two explicitly reused diagnostics and six small
constructed variations, not independent holdouts or 192 independent tasks.

Protected assembly services the same live controls first. Before selecting any
optional expansion, protect the oldest affordable current pending join by its
existing FIFO serial; activation and evaluator outcomes never enter this choice.
At most one protected opportunity per decision tranche. Execute it immediately,
then return to unchanged ordering. All expansions, including this join, retain the
same four flow steps and cooling. Negative attempts release unused credit. The
same unchanged join descriptor is not retried; a new binding invalidates derived
metadata without resetting episode work. Other joins retain ordinary ordering.

Query reserve: six typed current queries (five ordered premises and conclusion),
plus one exact producer read if nonresident, plus SIX exact-record preparation
reads. The preparation bound covers a producer and five distinct supports, and
also bounds other supported candidate kinds. Thus query affordability requires
12 credits for a resident producer or 13 if evicted, including remaining episode
and per-view query limits. This deliberately conservative bound is not reduced
when later outcomes look favorable. Affordability scans are bounded and timed.

The query reserve protects service, not proof. Before protecting, require room for
at least one response/tuple and the structural indivisible bundle plus current
pins. Actual unknown response bytes/cardinality, joint unions, combinations and
selected-bundle bytes are checked at the existing native/assembly/admission bounds.
A finite bounded attempt can terminate with an explicit response/joint/tuple or
pin-capacity result. Do not label an unqueried matching set absent or assert byte
feasibility from the producer alone. Five premise slots are always queried.

After the protected attempt, retain six preparation credits while an eligible
candidate remains. Optional query calls cannot spend them. At final selection,
release the local reservation to the unchanged prepare/pin operation and charge
actual exact rematerializations; unused credits are simply unused. The final
ranker remains first-discovery decision round plus the frozen semantic tie. No
record copies are hidden in the scheduler ledger and no new authority reservation,
coverage commitment or confidence is created. No affordable slot is explicitly
reported; ordinary bounded search may still proceed within existing limits.

Reservation/job instrumentation shares the existing trace and metadata bounds.
Measure job wait in intervening native calls, producer/tuple/commit milestones,
control costs, reserved/served/failed/stale slots and preparation exhaustion.
Separate field step time excluding guards, full guard-read time and event recording;
the former still contains kernel bookkeeping, not isolated floating-point arithmetic.
Keep inclusive and nested costs explicit; RSS, physical memory, standalone SQLite
fsync and physical observation latency are unmeasured. Full backing is unchanged.

Tests include identical public job streams, FIFO among alternatives, low-activation
service, actual native PLN, missing AND, adverse early work, all bounded failures,
charged rematerialization, stale exact identities/new producers, unchanged-join
suppression, contrary evidence, controls/monitoring and M12/conservation. Audit
native answers, field ledger, service reservations, no overspend, frozen semantic
parity, certificates/formulas and SQLite using existing tools. Run affected suites
at the measured revision; disclose broader inherited/omitted coverage precisely.

Publish all favorable, neutral and adverse outcomes. Attribute common gains to the
shared service rule, not flow. Stop after the small 2×3 publication. No adaptive
transport, learned retention, build caching, pressure tuning, storage redesign,
generalized recovery, larger cohort or automatic heuristic iteration.
