# What the decision sequences establish

Full public candidate ages/ties, expansion activation scores, native answers,
ordered supports, observations and loss changes are preserved for every pair in
`validation/paired-sequences.json` inside the archive. The original trace is the
source of truth. These are descriptions of complete recorded trajectories; no
single early divergence receives automatic credit for the whole terminal delta.

## Beneficial shared service

On `alternatives` at q16, frozen queue/local leave only three credits when the
first join job is selected, and frozen flow never selects it. All terminate at
certified/external loss 10. Each protected arm reserves a resident opportunity
at query 4, queries all five premise slots and the conclusion through query 10,
forms the complete `c-other` candidate, and keeps six credits available for
preparation. The candidate is resident, so actual rematerialization uses zero.
Original numerical certification succeeds, but loss remains unresolved until
execution and observations occur. All protected arms finish at 0/0.

At q48, frozen queue/local already succeed; protecting their join changes discovery
costs/order without improving terminal loss. Frozen flow still leaves joins pending
while leaf inspection consumes the tranche. Protected flow's improvement here is
an effect of the shared assembly service, also present in protected queue/local.
It is not evidence of incremental transport value.

## Adverse early inference and all-current acceptance

On `unproductive-early` at q16, frozen queue/local discover and adopt
`direct-received` first: strength .7, confidence .8. The authoritative decision
passes. Reserve, dispatch and exact-product observation keep certified loss at 10;
the first health observation moves external loss to 0, and the third moves certified

adopting that direct observation.

Protected queue/local instead give the earlier FIFO `a-low-yield` producer a slot
at query 4 and form its deduction by query 10. Native PLN and the joint certificate
succeed with strength `0.6799999999999999`, confidence `0.0014000000000000002`.
This is a valid low-confidence belief, not permission to deploy. Query preparation
protection then ends optional inspection at ten calls, with six credits still
held. The candidate's final FIFO age/tie ranking remains unchanged.

In the next tranche queue/local adopt the same direct report, but the weak current
alternative is still present. The all-current decision remains UNKNOWN; no reserve,
dispatch or product/health completion occurs. Certified/external loss stays 10/10.
The difference includes the new retained belief, subsequent report adoption and
unchanged authoritative acceptance policy, not simply the first job choice.
There are no stale selections in this case.

Protected flow also performs the weak deduction, then spends its next bounded
tranche on inspection and never adopts the direct report. At q16 its loss ties
protected queue/local at 10 despite a different sequence. At q48 protected
queue/local reach 0/0 while protected flow remains 10/10. Frozen flow is already
10/10 there: the shared rule has not fixed every later inspection bottleneck.
The unfavorable incremental flow result is retained without tuning.

## Relevant replacement and new native producer

On `refreshed-competing`, the first protected `z-route` deduction uses a coherent
snapshot and complete tuple, but a support is revoked/replaced with equal-valued
evidence at the proposal boundary. The existing postcheck returns STALE; no old
proposal is committed. Loss stays 10/10. Equality of truth values does not repair

At q16 the next protected attempt queries the refreshed native view and finds a
missing current premise. It reports `MISSING_PREMISES`, releases preparation credit
and ends unresolved under the original budget (queue/local use 15 queries; flow
uses 16). This is a negative complete join followed by bounded inspection, not a

At q48 all protected arms adopt the actual replacement report, then discover and

## Missing evidence is still unresolved

The reused `shared-intermediate` and constructed `missing-upstream` structures
remain at terminal 10/10 in all arms/settings. Protected joins can return actual
missing premise sets; other dependencies can remain unqueried at exhaustion.
Those are distinct outcomes. Frozen flow's q48 shared-intermediate trajectory even
performs two operations without closing the goal. Protecting one join cannot be
claimed to solve arbitrary upstream scheduling or ensure starvation freedom.

## Control, preparation and memory boundaries

The primary matrix keeps the existing control-first contract. The monitoring case
contains a wrong first product, later correct product, health service and reopening;
its failures are actual public observation results, not forced inference outcomes.
The dedicated uncertainty test uses a real lost-reply dispatch and reconciliation,
retains exactly one physical effect, and keeps goal loss unresolved without the
required observations.

Separate tests force producer and selected-support eviction through the original
counted LRU. A nonresident producer requires seven join queries; selected exact
records require six additional preparation reads. Another q16 test forces all six
selected records out after the join: preparation consumes the six protected credits
and finishes at exactly sixteen queries. Nothing is rescued from an uncounted
record cache. The primary eviction variation is reported as it ran; it is not
retuned to force rematerialization.

A stale guard abandons a protected slot and publishes no operation. Native response,
joint, tuple, metadata, query and pin/bundle limits remain separate. Affordability
is a conservative structural/query test; unknown response cardinality and bytes
are checked during actual bounded native assembly. The slot does not reserve an
authoritative resource, create coverage or authorize an otherwise blocked action.
