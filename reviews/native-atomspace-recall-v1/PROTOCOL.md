cohort, measurements, auditor correction, failures and reviews byte-for-byte.

rules, current/historical numerical records, received reports, probes, independence

closure. Query membership comes from native incoming sets and ordered outgoing
relations. Typed exact source IDs may decode returned records. There is no parallel
